About Ropebreak

I'm not a coffee expert. I'm not a beer critic. I've never worked a single shift behind a bar, and my only wrestling qualification is watching an unreasonable amount of it since I was a kid.

What I am is someone who spends most of his week in meetings, ticking boxes, and generally functioning like a very tired spreadsheet. Ropebreak is what happens on the other side of that — coffee shops, pubs, the odd pint, and whatever wildcard thing manages to grab my attention along the way.

No jargon, no tasting notes borrowed from a textbook, no pretending I know more than I do. Just honest, occasionally unhinged opinions from a normal guy who likes writing more than he probably should.

I crave a creative outlet, but have very few creative skills. It's a conundrum. "How did you solve this riddle?!" I hear you cry. Writing things. Reviewing things. From someone who just enjoys things without being a pro in them, whilst also being okay at articulating things in ways people enjoy. My imposter syndrome wants you to enjoy this more than you know.

No monetisation plan. Just a break from the 9-to-5, written down.
