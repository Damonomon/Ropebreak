Ropebreak

Privacy

The Ropebreak app and website don't ask for an account, don't track you, don't show ads and don't collect any personal information.

The app downloads the latest reviews from this website and keeps a copy on your phone so you can read them offline. That copy never leaves your device.

If you tap "Share review" or "View on map", your phone's own share menu or map service takes over, under that service's privacy terms.
